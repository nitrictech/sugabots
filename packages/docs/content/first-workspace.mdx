---
title: Your first workspace
description: Name it, connect a provider, make your first bot and invite your members.
---

The first time you sign in, Sugabots walks you through five short steps. Only
an admin can finish them, and whoever creates a workspace is its owner.

## The five steps

<Steps>

1. Name your workspace

   Something like "The Smiths' bots" or "Acme Support". You can rename it later
   in **Settings → General**.

2. Connect a provider

   Bots need a model to think with, from a provider. Choose one, such as
   **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** or **OpenRouter**, and paste an API key; sign in
   with **ChatGPT**; or give Ollama's server URL if it runs on your own
   machine. Then choose a model: your first bot thinks with it. You can switch
   on more, or add other providers, later in **Settings → Models**.

3. Make your first bot

   Give it a shape, some eyes, a colour and a name. Everyone in the workspace
   can talk to it. You can [make more bots](/docs/bots) for particular jobs
   once you're in, and your own
   [Personal Assistant](/docs/bots#personal-assistants) is already there.

4. Bring your friends

   Type in some email addresses and Sugabots sends each member an invitation.
   They join as editors. Invitations expire after two days, and you can
   **Resend** them from **Settings → Members**. Or **Skip for now**.

   <Tip title="Sharing with others">

   If you want to share Sugabots with others, you'll need to get it
   [hosted](/docs/deploy-with-docker).
   Running it on your own computer, as in the Quickstart? Skip this step for
   now.

   </Tip>

5. Start chatting

   Your bot is ready, and asks you in a chat what it's for.

</Steps>

## After workspace setup

<Tip title="Pick a system model">

Sugabots uses a [model of its own](/docs/models#system-model) to
compact long chats for a bot with no model of its own. It starts on
the model you picked during setup. A fast, cheap model is best, so you may want
to change it in **Settings → Models**, under **Defaults → Default for system bots**.

</Tip>

A few good next steps:

- [Make more bots](/docs/bots), each with its own job and model.
- [Create a project](/docs/projects) for a group, and invite members to it once
  your server is hosted.
- [Add a connector](/docs/connectors), like Linear or Notion, so a bot can
  work with your apps.
