title: Usage
description: See what your bots cost, a month at a time, broken down by bot, model or pod.
---

The owner and admins can see what the workspace's models have cost in
**Settings → Usage**. Members and viewers don't see the page.

## What it shows

Each month's total, with a bar for every day, then the month broken down three
ways. Step back to earlier months with the arrows beside the month's name.

- **Bot**: what each bot's replies and routine runs cost. The work
  Sugabots does behind the scenes, such as summarising, titling and compacting
  conversations, is listed as **System agents**.
- **Model**: what each model cost, whichever bots use it.
- **Pod**: what each pod's bots cost.

## How it's worked out

The amounts are estimates, from each provider's published prices and the
number of tokens each request used. A request Sugabots can't price, such as one
to a model whose price it doesn't know, is counted but not included in the total.

Your provider bills you directly, so its own dashboard has the exact figure. To
spend less, give a bot a cheaper model in its settings, or pick a fast, cheap
model for the [work behind the scenes](/docs/models#behind-the-scenes-model).
