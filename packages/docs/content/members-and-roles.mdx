---
title: Members and roles
description: Who can join a workspace, and what its owner, admins, editors and viewers can do.
---

Everyone in a workspace has a role: owner, admin, editor or viewer. The role decides
what they can change. A bot's visibility and their project memberships decide what they can see.

## Who gets in

Whoever creates a workspace owns it. Anyone can sign up to a new server, so
its first account can be made. After that, sign-up is closed and members arrive
by invitation, unless the server sets
[`SIGNUP_MODE`](/docs/configuration#access) to `referral` or `open`.

To invite members, go to **Settings → Members** and choose **Invite members**.
Type their email addresses, separated by commas, and pick a **Role**. Each member
gets an email with a link to join.

<Tip>

Invitations expire after two days. Anyone who joins gets their own
[Personal Assistant](/docs/bots#personal-assistants) straight away.

</Tip>

### Referral links

On a server whose `SIGNUP_MODE` is `referral`, everyone has a link that
lets somebody new make an account. Find yours in **Settings → Profile** under
**Share your referral link**. Whoever signs up with it sets up a workspace of their own;
it doesn't add them to yours.

The link keeps working until you choose **Reset link**, which replaces it and
stops the old one working.

## Roles

There are four. Pick one to see what it can do.

<RoleMatrix />

Everyone can talk to the workspace's public bots and find its public projects.
Private bots and private projects are for their members. The workspace's owner
and admins are members of every private bot and every project members made.
Members can talk to the private bots they belong to. Members with any role
except viewer can add or remove members of those bots.

In a project they belong to, editors can add or remove bots and members,
start automation runs and use the desktop. The workspace's owner and admins
can do all of that and are the only ones who can change project settings,
choose its lead bot or turn on a bot's own connector there.

Everyone can make a Personal Assistant, use its desktop and manage, delete
and start automation runs for their own personal bots. Only the creator can
talk to a personal bot and read its chats. The workspace's owner and admins
can see that it exists, switch off its connectors and automations, or delete
it.

Each workspace has exactly one owner: whoever created it. The owner can do
everything an admin can, and is the only one who can make someone an admin, stop
them being one, or remove them.

## Leaving and removing

Open a member in **Settings → Members** to change their role or remove them.
Removing someone takes away their access to the workspace and every project in
it. Their Personal Assistants and their chats are deleted, but the projects and
other bots they made stay, and their personal bots' automations are switched off.

Sugabots checks a member's access when a request, bot reply or automation run
starts. If the member loses access after it starts, the work can finish, even
if the bot was waiting for approval. New requests, replies, runs and approval
decisions use the member's current access. If access was lost, live updates
and open desktop views stop the next time Sugabots checks.

The owner can't be removed or lose their role, and can't leave. To hand the
workspace on, the owner opens another member in **Settings → Members** and makes
them the owner. The new owner takes over, and the old one stays on as an
admin.
