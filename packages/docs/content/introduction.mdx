title: What is Sugabots?
description: A workspace where people and AI bots work together, in threads and projects.
---

Sugabots is a workspace where some of your teammates are bots. You give each bot
a job, a model to think with and the tools it may use. Then you, your team, your
family or your flatmates work with them, one to one or together in projects.

It's open source and self-hosted. Your chats, settings and API keys live in your
own database, and messages only go to the model providers you choose.

## The workspace, taken apart

- **Bots** are AI teammates. A **public** bot is for everyone in the workspace;
  a **private** one only for the person who made it. Each has its own threads,
  files, computer, connections and automations.
- A bot's **threads** are its conversations, each about one piece of work.
  Whoever can talk to the bot can read and join its threads.
- **Projects** are long-running pieces of work shared by many people and many
  bots, made of their threads, with the files made there.
- **Your Personal Assistant** is a bot only you can talk to. It knows the
  workspace, and points you to the right bot or project.

## What it looks like

Here's the Family project's Lisbon trip thread on a Thursday evening. Mum asks for flights, and
Trip Planner gets Budget Keeper's opinion before it books anything.

<Chat title="Lisbon trip">
  <Say from="mum">Can someone please sort the Lisbon flights before the prices jump again</Say>
  <Say from="you">On it. @trip-planner, find us something Friday after 5, and check it fits the holiday pot</Say>
  <Say from="tripPlanner" collaboratesWith="budgetKeeper">Found two. Checking them against the holiday pot.</Say>
  <Say from="tripPlanner">Two options under €180 each. The 18:40 lands in time for dinner, and Budget Keeper says four seats still leave €220 in the pot.</Say>
  <Say from="dad">18:40. Nobody is doing a 6am flight again.</Say>
</Chat>

Three things happened there:

- **Everyone saw the same thread.** A project's threads belong to everyone in
  it, people and bots.
- **One bot asked another.** Trip Planner wrote to Budget Keeper on its own, and
  anyone can open that exchange and read it.
- **Nothing was booked without asking.** The travel app's connection was set to
  Ask, so each tool the bot used waited for a person to allow it.
