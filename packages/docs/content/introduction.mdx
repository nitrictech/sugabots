---
title: What is Sugabots?
description: A workspace where members and AI bots work together, in chats and projects.
---

Sugabots is a workspace where some of your teammates are bots. You give each bot
a job, a model to think with and the tools it may use. Then you, your team, your
family or your flatmates work with them, one to one or together in projects.

It's open source and self-hosted. Your chats, settings and API keys live in your
own database, and messages only go to the model providers you choose.

## The workspace, taken apart

- **Bots** are AI teammates. A **public** bot is for everyone in the workspace.
  A **private** bot is for its members. A **personal** bot is for the member who
  made it and works with their permissions. Each bot has its own chats, files,
  computer, connectors and automations.
- A bot's **chats** are its chats, each about one piece of work.
  Whoever can talk to the bot can read and join its chats.
- **Projects** are long-running pieces of work shared by many members and many
  bots, made of their chats, with the files made there.
- **Your Personal Assistants** are personal bots that know the workspace, and
  point you to the right bot or project. You get one when you join, and can make
  more.

## What it looks like

Here's the Family project's Lisbon trip chat on a Thursday evening. Mum asks for flights, and
Trip Planner gets Budget Keeper's opinion before it books anything.

<Chat title="Lisbon trip">
  <Say from="mum">Can someone please sort the Lisbon flights before the prices jump again</Say>
  <Say from="you">On it. @trip-planner, find us something Friday after 5, and check it fits the holiday pot</Say>
  <Say from="tripPlanner">Found two under €180 each. @budget-keeper, do four seats fit the holiday pot?</Say>
  <Say from="budgetKeeper">They do. Four seats still leave €220 in the pot.</Say>
  <Say from="tripPlanner">Then the 18:40: it lands in time for dinner.</Say>
  <Say from="dad">18:40. Nobody is doing a 6am flight again.</Say>
</Chat>

Three things happened there:

- **Everyone saw the same chat.** A project's chats belong to everyone in
  it, members and bots.
- **One bot asked another.** Trip Planner asked Budget Keeper on its own, by
  mentioning it, and Budget Keeper answered in the chat for everyone to read.
- **Nothing was booked without asking.** The travel app's connector was set to
  Ask, so each tool the bot used waited for a member to allow it.
