A bot is an AI teammate. Each one has its own instructions and model, and its
own threads, files, computer, connections and automations. Its threads are kept
in a hidden project of its own, which it leads alone, so they work just as a
[project's](/docs/projects) do; only you never see that project itself.

## Make a bot

Owners, admins and members can make bots. You choose:

- **Colour** and **Eyes**: how it looks. There are eight colours and five pairs
  of eyes.
- **Name**: what people call it. Its handle comes from the name, so
  "Trip Planner" is `@trip-planner`. Names and handles are unique across the
  workspace.
- **Public** or **private**: who can talk to it. See
  [below](#public-and-private).

A new bot starts on the workspace's [default model](/docs/models#default-model).
If there's none, connect one in **Settings → Models** first. Bots can't think
without one.

A new bot is pinned to your sidebar, and opens in a setup thread: it interviews
you about the work it's for, then writes its own instructions. You can read and
change them afterwards in its settings.

## Public and private

- A **public** bot is for everyone in the workspace. Anyone can find it, talk to
  it, and see its threads and files. Only public bots can be added to
  [projects](/docs/projects).
- A **private** bot is for its creator alone. Nobody else can talk to it or see
  its threads. The owner and admins see that it exists, but not its threads.

Other bots can still [ask a private bot for help](/docs/conversations#bots-asking-bots).
Making a bot private takes it out of every project it was in. A bot that
[leads a project](/docs/projects#lead-bot) can't be made private until another
bot leads it.

## What a bot has

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Threads | Its conversations, each about one piece of work. See [Conversations](/docs/conversations) |
| Files | Files uploaded or made in its threads |
| Computer | Its own machine, where it runs commands and keeps files in its threads. It's paused when idle. In a project's threads it uses the [project's computer](/docs/projects#computer) instead |
| Connections | The apps and APIs it works with, and its own GitHub app. In a project's threads it also uses the [project's](/docs/projects#connections), which win where both have one with the same name. See [Connections](/docs/connections) |
| Automations | Work it does on its own, on a routine, such as every weekday at 9, or when something triggers it. See [Automations](/docs/automations) |

Above a bot's threads, **Lately** says in a few sentences what it has been doing: the Summariser writes it from the summaries of its newest threads each time one of them changes. Your Personal Assistant reads it too, to know what each bot is up to.

## Tell it what to do

Its creator, the owner and admins can change a bot's settings:

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Description | One line on what the bot is for. Other bots read it to decide whether to ask it for help |
| Model | What it thinks with. Each bot can use a different one |
| Instructions | How it should work. It follows these in every thread and project |
| Tools | Which built-in tools it may use |
| Visibility | Public or private |

<Tip title="Writing instructions">

Write them like a note to a new teammate: what it's for, how to sound, and what
to never do. You have up to 20,000 characters. Everyone who can see a public bot
can read its instructions, so describe the job, not the person asking.

</Tip>

For example:

```md title="Instructions"
You plan family trips. Find options that suit everyone, and always check the
price with @budget-keeper before suggesting anything.

Keep replies short. Nobody wants to read an essay on a phone.

Never book anything without asking first.
```

## Your sidebar

The sidebar shows the bots you've pinned, under your
[Personal Assistant](#your-personal-assistant). The rest of the workspace's
bots are a browse away. Pin or unpin any bot you can see.

## Your Personal Assistant

Everyone has a **Personal Assistant**, made for them when they join, and always
first in their sidebar. Only you can talk to yours or see its threads, admins
included.

It knows the workspace: the projects, the bots you can reach and what each has
been doing lately, and what is waiting on you, such as unread mentions,
approvals and failing automations. Ask it what needs you, or how something in
Sugabots works. It points you to the right bot, and starts a thread with it for
you when you agree. It can also make a project, join one, add a bot to a
project or pin a bot for you: each waits for your approval before it happens. You can change
its look and add connections to it. Its instructions are looked after by
Sugabots, so you can't edit them.

## Remove a bot

**Delete bot** is at the bottom of its settings. Its threads, files, automations
and connections go with it, and that can't be undone. Its creator, the owner and
admins can delete it, once another bot leads any project it leads. A Personal
Assistant can't be deleted.
