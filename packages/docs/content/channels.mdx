A channel is where a group works on something together: a team, a family, a
flat, a launch. It has its people and its bots, a **main chat** everyone shares,
**threads** for focused conversations beside it, and the **files** made there.

<ChannelMap />

## Public and private

When you make a channel, you choose who can find it:

- **Public** channels are listed in the channel directory. Anyone in the
  workspace can find one and join it, but nobody is added without choosing to.
- **Private** channels are invitation only. Nobody sees them unless they're in
  them.

The owner and admins are in every channel, public or private.

## Make a channel

Owners, admins and members can make channels from the channel directory. Give
it a name like "Support", a line saying what it's for, and choose public or
private. You're its first member.

The sidebar lists the channels you're in, each with its threads under it.

## People

Anyone in a channel can invite people from the workspace, or remove them, and
anyone can leave. The owner and admins can't be removed from a channel or leave
it, because they're in every one.

Viewers can join public channels, read and take part, but they can't invite or
remove anyone.

## Bots

Anyone in a channel, except viewers, can add bots to it and take them out. Only
[public bots](/docs/bots#public-and-private) can be added, and a bot that's
made private leaves every channel it was in.

Bots in a channel answer only when asked. Mention one or more by handle and
each of them answers at once, or reply to a bot's message and that bot answers.
A message that does neither is for the people. See
[Conversations](/docs/conversations#who-answers).

## Main chat and threads

The main chat is the channel's own conversation, with all its people and bots.
It's where work is discussed and where threads are started.

A thread is one focused conversation beside the main chat, such as one bug, one
trip or one week's report. Everyone in the channel sees every thread, and the
sidebar lists them under the channel.

## Files

A channel's **Files** are the ones uploaded or made in its main chat and
threads. Everyone in the channel can open them. The owner and admins can delete
them.

## Rename or delete

The owner and admins can rename a channel, change what it's for, and delete it.

<Careful title="Deleting a channel">

**Delete channel** removes its main chat, threads and files. Its bots stay in
the workspace. It can't be undone.

</Careful>
