title: People and roles
description: Who can join a workspace, and what its owner, admins, members and viewers can do.
---

Everyone in a workspace has a role: owner, admin, member or viewer. The role decides
what they can change, and pods decide what they can see.

## Who gets in

Whoever creates a workspace owns it. Anyone can sign up to a new installation, so
its first account can be made. After that, sign-up is closed and people arrive
by invitation, unless the installation sets
[`SIGNUP_MODE`](/docs/configuration#access) to `referral` or `open`.

To invite people, go to **Settings → Members** and choose **Invite people**.
Type their email addresses, separated by commas, and pick a **Role**. Each person
gets an email with a link to join.

<Tip>

Invites expire after two days. Anyone who joins gets their own
[Personal pod](/docs/pods#your-personal-pod) straight away.

</Tip>

### Referral links

On an installation whose `SIGNUP_MODE` is `referral`, everyone has a link that
lets somebody new make an account. Find yours in **Settings → Profile** under
**Invite to Sugabots**. Whoever signs up with it sets up a workspace of their own;
it doesn't add them to yours.

The link keeps working until you choose **Reset link**, which replaces it and
stops the old one working.

## Roles

There are four. Pick one to see what it can do.

Members and viewers only see the pods they've been added to. The owner and admins
can reach every shared pod, but nobody can see into someone else's Personal pod.

Each workspace has exactly one owner: whoever created it. The owner can do
everything an admin can, and is the only one who can make someone an admin, stop
them being one, or remove them.

## Leaving and removing

Open a person in **Settings → Members** to change their role or remove them.
Removing someone takes away their access to the workspace and every pod in it. Their Personal pod
and its conversations are deleted, but pods they made stay.

The owner can't be removed or lose their role, and can't leave. To hand the
workspace on, the owner opens another person in **Settings → Members** and makes
them the owner. The new owner takes over, and the old one stays on as an
admin.
