title: People and roles
description: Who can join a workspace, and what admins, members and viewers can do.
---

Everyone in a workspace has a role: admin, member or viewer. The role decides
what they can change, and pods decide what they can see.

## Who gets in

The first person to sign up on an installation owns it, and becomes the admin of
the workspace they create. After that, sign-up is by invitation only, unless the
installation turns on [`ALLOW_OPEN_SIGNUP`](/docs/configuration#access).

To invite people, go to **Settings → Members** and choose **Invite people**.
Type their email addresses, separated by commas, and pick a **Role**. Each person
gets an email with a link to join.

<Tip>

Invites expire after two days. Anyone who joins gets their own
[Personal pod](/docs/pods#your-personal-pod) straight away.

</Tip>

## Roles

There are three. Pick one to see what it can do.

<RoleMatrix />

Members and viewers only see the pods they've been added to. Admins can reach
every shared pod, but nobody can see into someone else's Personal pod.

## Leaving and removing

Open a person in **Settings → Members** to change their role or remove them.
Removing someone takes away their access to the workspace and every pod in it. Their Personal pod
and its conversations are deleted, but pods they made stay.

A workspace always keeps at least one admin: the only admin can't be removed or
lose the admin role.
