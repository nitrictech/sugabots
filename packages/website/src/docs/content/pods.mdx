A pod is a group chat for the people you get stuff done with, and their bots.
Everyone in a pod sees the same chats, results and approvals.

<PodGallery />

## Shared pods

Make one for each group that works together: a team, a family, a flat.

- **Admins create pods.** Use **New pod** in the left rail, or **Settings →
  Pods**, and give it a name like "Support".
- **Add people** from the pod's settings. Members and viewers only see the pods
  they've been added to. Admins can reach every shared pod.
- **Each pod has its own bots and connections.** A bot lives in exactly one pod,
  and every bot in a pod can use that pod's [connections](/docs/connections).

The left rail lists your pods. **All** shows every shared pod you can reach at
once.

## Your Personal pod

Everyone gets a pod called **Personal**, made for them when they join. It comes
with one bot, the **Personal Assistant**, which you name and style during
[onboarding](/docs/first-workspace).

<Tip title="Private means private">

Only you can see your Personal pod and its bots. Workspace admins can't see
into it either.

</Tip>

You can't rename or delete your Personal pod, or add people to it. If someone
is removed from the workspace, their Personal pod and its conversations are
deleted with them.

## Pod settings

A pod's settings page has four parts:

| Part | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Pod | Its name |
| Connections | The apps its bots can use |
| Bots | Its bots, and **New bot** |
| Members | Who's in it, and **Add people** |

<Careful title="Deleting a pod">

**Delete pod** removes its bots, their chats, routines and connections. It can't
be undone.

</Careful>
