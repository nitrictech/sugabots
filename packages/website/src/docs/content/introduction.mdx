---
title: What is Sugabots?
description: A workspace where people and AI bots work together, in shared spaces called pods.
---

Sugabots is a group chat where some of the members are bots. You give each bot a
job, a model to think with and the tools it may use. Then you, your team, your
family or your flatmates talk to them together.

It's open source and self-hosted. Your chats, settings and API keys live in your
own database, and messages only go to the model providers you choose.

## A pod, taken apart

Everything happens in a **pod**: a shared space for a group of people and their
bots. Pick a part to see how it fits.

Everyone also has a **Personal** pod that only they can see. Nobody else can see
it, admins included.

## What it looks like

Here's the Family pod on a Thursday evening. Mum asks for flights, and
Trip Planner gets Budget Keeper's opinion before it books anything.

<Chat title="Family · Trip Planner">
  <Say from="mum">Can someone please sort the Lisbon flights before the prices jump again</Say>
  <Say from="you">On it. Trip Planner, find us something Friday after 5, and check it fits the holiday pot</Say>
  <Activity bots={["tripPlanner", "budgetKeeper"]}>Trip Planner collaborated with Budget Keeper</Activity>
  <Say from="tripPlanner">Two options under €180 each. The 18:40 lands in time for dinner, and Budget Keeper says four seats still leave €220 in the pot.</Say>
  <Say from="dad">18:40. Nobody is doing a 6am flight again.</Say>
</Chat>

Three things happened there:

- **Everyone saw the same chat.** A bot's chat belongs to the pod, not to one
  person.
- **One bot asked another.** Trip Planner wrote to Budget Keeper on its own, and
  anyone can open that exchange and read it.
- **Nothing was booked without asking.** When a bot wants to change something
  through a tool, it waits for a person to allow it.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Pods | Shared spaces for people and their bots |
| Bots | Agents with a job, a model and a face |
| Conversations | One chat per bot, plus the threads that spin off it |
| Tools | What bots can do beyond talking, and when they ask first |
| Connections | Apps like Linear and Notion, plugged into a pod |
| Models | The providers your bots think with |
| Routines | Jobs bots do on a schedule or when called |
